# World
Corporate dystopia

1. magical otherworld is a pyramid, regular world is a ~~circle~~ flat because it's the bottom of the pyramid. People think it's round, but it actually is a government conspiracy (maybe just for shits and gigs)
2. Magic serves no obvious role in society, technology is the normal level. Game takes place in the 90's
3. New York City
	1. Ethan - **Staten Island**. After the invasion of Cuba, the Cubans formed a beachhead on Staten Island and it was never recovered, so it's kind of a ww1 no-man's land. It's like a previous warzone, not active. Local hooligan spot
	2. Shork - **Central Park**. Central park is the same but an untouched indian jungle. Andrew Jackson made it like that in the 1800s to punish new york (in response to complaints about the trail of tears), and the NYPD is constantly dealing with wild animals escaping. The central park zoo is real but it's a fortress for the NYPD
	3. James - **Broadway**. Instead of a big billboard kind of thing, broadway never changed and it's a historic district with all the same untouched theaters. They have newsboys reenactors with a strong union and everything, good journalistic integrity.
4. Historical Events
	1. Ethan - **Andrew Jackson gets mad hatter's disease**. Injured as a young boy, received experimental mercury surgery which leaked into his brain causing him to make a series of rash decisions during his reign as president.
	2. Shork - **Cuba successfully invades Florida**. The US has little to no presence in Florida and does not recognize its independence, but the government can't do anything about it because they don't have the political muster.
	3. James - Teddy Roosevelt is unanimously elected god emperor of america but he disappears mysteriously in the central park jungle during his 8th term. He is succeeded by Franklin Roosevelt who reinstates term limits and reestablishes american democracy.
	4. Ethan - **Cuban invasion ends after the battle of Staten Island**. Island is left as historical site.
	5. Shork - Great Divide from Lonesome Road exists due to nuclear testing, but las vegas is fine. Major humanitarian crisis being hidden by the US government
	6. Ethan - Complications in the development of film technology, causing its popularization to happen later, thus live performance is more popular. Lots of films in the real world were made as plays
	7. James - China won the opium war and never fell to communism. Shen Yeun is real and has actual political leverage in china and in the us broadway scene.
	8. Shork - the ethiopian orthodox christianity is more popular worldwide and and the holy site is genuinely holy
5. Mysteries and Enigmas
	1. James - What happened to Teddy Roosevelt's body. It was never recovered, and no one can tame the jungle, people set out to try and find it sometimes, always failed.
	2. Ethan - What happened to Isla Sorna? ([jurassic park book](https://archive.org/details/jurassic-park-michael-crichton_202104/mode/2up) is canon, ethan says listen to the audiobook) Random island got firebombed for seemingly no reason (to kill all the dinosaurs)
	3. Shork - nobody knows why micronesia is physically geographically growing at a rapid pace (not due to volcanic activity; conspiracy theory that it's atlantis rising from the sea)
	4. James - we still don't know what happened to hitler's body
	5. Shork - people think of nuclear power like semiconductors where people just don't know how it works so we kinda just do rituals. Nuclear stuff is in the great divide
6. Threats
	1. Ethan - Central park was never really in hand but it's getting more out of hand
	2. Shork - Cuba is militarizing
	3. Shork - existence of new jersey >:( Otherworld new jersey is the root of all evil, regular new jersey is fine
	4. James - without proper understanding of how radiation works, companies are rapidly exhausting our fuel reserves (basically like the setup for ww3 as explained in fallout 4)
	5. Shork - big money powerbrokers are starting a nuclear smuggling ring

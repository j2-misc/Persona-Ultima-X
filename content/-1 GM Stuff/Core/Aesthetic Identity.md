# Musical Identity
Each PC should have different themes or musical motifs. Since there's only three players, I think the easiest way to do this is rip most of the songs for each player from one of the persona games.

> [!WARNING] Music is important!
> Music is core to the identity of each persona game and as such great care and attention should be paid to what songs play when/where, and why. Skimping on this will not ruin the game, but it will lessen the potential impact it can have on players.
# Color Coding
The game should be green, but not bc it's not taken by the three modern persona games, but because green is the color of tartarus in persona 3 and this game is supposed to be about apocalyptic doomerism and so having everything be green like that.

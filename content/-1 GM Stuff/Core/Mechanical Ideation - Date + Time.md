Within the narrative, PCs will spend most of their time working, attending classes, and doing downtime activities.
# Days and Spending Time
Most of the PCs' time is spent automatically at their jobs, classes, or enduring the odd fatigue of NEEThood. The remainder of their time can be spent however they wish. Like in the Persona video games, certain days have special events which may occupy the whole day and not allow for any downtime (e.g. exam week, extended trips, certain special events). PCs should typically only have **one** free time slot per day. PCs may have a maximum of **two** free time slots in a single day, but these should be limited to days where they have a unique amount of free time (like on Sundays) or carry consequences.

As PCs are individuals, how they choose to spend their time does not necessarily affect the time or availability of other PCs.

> [!NOTE] Persona 5 Time Slots
> In Persona 5 Royal, the player typically has 2 free time slots per day. This works well for that game, but since we're removing Social Stats and there are multiple PCs, I feel it's best to limit everyone to just one activity per day except on special days where two is permissible.
# Mutual Exclusivity
Confidants (see [[Mechanical Ideation - Social Links|Social Links]]) should have regular schedules sometimes interrupted by increased or decreased availability (e.g. a Confidant may spontaneously get some time off work, or suddenly have to cover a co-worker's shift). Confidant schedules should overlap and force players to choose whether they'd rather spend time with Confidant A or Confidant B. It could actually be even super interesting if a Confidant is occupied by another PC.

Confidants should regularly message PCs when they are available. 
# Round Robin
In order to ensure everyone's having fun and not getting too bored, there should be a consistent turn order to who's doing what and when. This order should be optimized to keep the player with the juiciest thing they want to do last. For example, if Player A wants to see the last SL rank with a Confidant, they should be the last to go, and that turn order should be consistent to guarantee that.

Certain activities might occupy the time slots of multiple party members.
# Regular Downtime Activities
In addition to spending time with Confidants, PCs should be able to spend free time for regular downtime activities like crafting/invention as described by the rules of Fabula Ultima.
# Miscellaneous Downtime Activities
PCs should be able to spend free time doing persona type miscellaneous shit that grants access to things or gives little buffs. For example:
- Reading a magazine that unlocks a new area in the city where a new SL could be formed
- Watching a TV show or movie might give them something to talk about with a Confidant or with an NPC to start a SL
- Working part time to earn more money (maybe have an associated minigame?)
- Or anything else if the players have something specific in mind, such as investigating an aspect of a target

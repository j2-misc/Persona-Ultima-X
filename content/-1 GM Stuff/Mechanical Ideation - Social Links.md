How do I want to implement social links?
# Abstract
- SLs are the relationships PCs have with NPCs
- SLs should provide unique mechanical bonuses to that PC/NPC pairing
- If all PCs should have an SL with one NPC, consider making it a Group SL where bonuses affect the entire party (such as for a navigator)
	- Though having a Group SL with an NPC does not necessarily prevent a PC from having an Individual SL with that same NPC if they have a unique individual relationship (e.g. all PCs might have a GSL with the Navigator, but one PC might have a particularly close relationship with them, warranting an SL of their own)
- SLs are Soft Locked (limited mechanical bonuses) by main quest progression
- SLs are Hard Locked (refuse to participate in narrative) by SL Keys relevant to their specific character development
# What is a Social Link
Well first let's analyze what social links are meant to represent in the abstract. Social Links represent bonds between people. They represent how the people we spend our limited time with are the people who help us overcome our greatest obstacles, which we wouldn't otherwise be able to alone. So social links need to give players tangible, significant bonuses. These bonuses should also be relevant to the adventure so they get use.

Oh my god, I could treat them like magic items or rather like boons. And on that note they should be individualized as well. That is to say, the bonuses provided to each character by their individual social links should be different based on the kind of character that player is playing and the kind of relationship they have with that NPC.

Also for definition purposes, a **Social Link** is the relationship between a PC and an NPC, rather than being synonymous with the NPC. An NPC which a PC has a Social Link with is called a **Confidant**. That is to say, PCs can all individually have different SLs with the same NPC. While these may narratively affect one another, they are different SLs and therefore should provide different bonuses to the players. That said, if all players have a SL with one NPC, consider making a Group SL, where the bonuses affect the group overall rather than individual PCs.

> *You spent time alone with \[NPC]...*
# Ideas for Implementation
Some ideas for SL boons:
- Affect fabula point economy
- Store discount/increased item selection (overpowered items)
- Expedited or cheaper crafting
- Enhanced class skill

And for Group SL boons:
- Reliable talent for Study checks
# Social Link Progression
SLs shouldn't be gatekept by anything like social stats, because I feel like those wouldn't translate particularly well into Fabula Ultima. I think they should be gatekept by quests, though. In order to earn SL boons, in addition to just spending significant downtime with an NPC, PCs need to do side quests for them in order to advance their storyline and see their SL to the end.

Also unlike the Persona video games where you can max out a social link really quickly, I want all SLs to only be paced alongside the main quest. That way there's no narrative weirdness where there's a climax of one character's whole arc before you've even heard about kaneshiro. I don't want to necessarily limit the narrative possibilities of spending more time together, but I think I want to limit mechanical progression by the progression of the main quest while only soft limiting narrative progression. Meaning, if a PC wants to hang out with an NPC, they might not get anything mechanically useful, but they'll get a fun interaction and maybe NPCs could take a more active role in the main plot at the discretion of the PCs. Like there could be more moments like where Ren calls Takemi to diagnose Futaba, like there could just be more of that without the NPCs being necessarily being directly involved in the party's actions. Narrative progression should only be hard locked (e.g. NPC refusing to even spend time with PCs) by SL specific requests. Let's call them Keys.
# Regarding Bonds
Keep bonds resulting from a SL dynamic, and should shift with its narrative, but other than that just feel 'em out like normal for Fabula Ultima. Spending free time with a Confidant counts as a Resting Scene and therefore PCs can create a new bond, add an emotion to an existing bond, or change an emotion associated with that bond.
# Social Links Between PCs
Rather than being like Confidants where PCs have to spend time to advance the SL, SLs between PCs advance spontaneously whenever it feels appropriate. These should also grant significant mechanical boons (such as gaining the Protect Guardian Skill but only for that character)
